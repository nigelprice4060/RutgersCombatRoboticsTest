Welcome to the official website of Rutgers University's Combat Robotics team!

Welcome to Rutgers Combat Robotics!

We are a student organization at Rutgers University that builds combat robots and competes in competitions. We are always looking for sponsors to help us fund our projects. If you are a business and would like to sponsor us, please click the button below.

Our Competitions

We compete in various competitions throughout the year, including the BattleBots competition and the Robot Wars competition. Our team has won several awards and accolades for our robots, and we are always looking to improve and innovate.

Merchandise

We also sell merchandise to help fund our team. You can find our merchandise on our online store, which includes t-shirts, hoodies, and other items. All proceeds from our merchandise sales go directly to funding our team and our projects.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or would like to get in touch with us, please feel free to contact us through our email or social media channels. We are always happy to hear from our supporters and fans!
