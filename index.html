Welcome to Rutgers Combat Robotics!

We are the Rutgers University Combat Robotics Team, officially partnered with the Rutgers Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineering. Our plan is to compete in both the National Havoc Robot League and the Garden State Combat Robotics League. Our team currently consists of 70+ dedicated engineers, computer scientists, and other professionals. Currently, we design and build 3lb combat robots to compete against other teams' robots in a 3-minute battle.
Our business subdivision drives our social media and company outreach, building our community, and sharing our love robots. Our technical roadmap includes expanding into higher weight classes and fielding multiple robots at once, along with creating workshops to help educate our newer members on how to create robots — building a pipeline of engineers who can carry the team forward for years to come.

Competitions

We participate in several national and regional competitions for combat robots, including the NHRL and GSCRL competitions.
