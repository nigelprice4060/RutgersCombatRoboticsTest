Welcome to Rutgers Combat Robotics!

We are the Rutgers University Combat Robotics Team, officially partnered with the Rutgers Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineering. Our plan is to compete in both the National Havoc Robot League and Garden State Combat Robotics League competitions annually. Our team consists of 70+ dedicated engineers, computer scientists, and other professionals spanning multiple fields of study. Currently, we design and build 3lb combat robots to compete against other robots in 3 minute battles, clips of which you can find on our Instagram.
Our business subdivision drives our social media and business connections in order to build a community eager to share our love of robots. Our technical roadmap includes expanding into higher weight classes and fielding multiple robots at once, along with creating workshops to help educate prospective STEM enthusiasts on how to create robots, building a pipeline of engineers who can carry the team forward for years to come.

Competitions

We participate in several national and regional competitions for combat robots, including the NHRL and GSCRL competitions.
